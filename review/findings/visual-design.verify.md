- **VIS-03: CORRECTED.** The evidence holds. Recomputed ratios: white on #FF4F00 is 3.30:1, white on #FF5A14 is 3.12:1, #6B7385 on #F3F4F7 is 4.32:1 and on #F5F6F9 is 4.40:1. The light-theme fix also checks out: white on #D63F00 is 4.59:1, and #5E6677 is 5.24:1 on `--bg` and 5.33:1 on `--panel`. **The dark-theme fix is wrong on both options:**
